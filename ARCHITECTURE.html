System architecture · THE HUMAN OS™ programme

How Nitienaa Rao works, end to end

One Next.js application serves the public website, the coach dashboard, the learner portal and the admin panel. It saves everything in one database and hands each job to a specialist service: payments, files, email, WhatsApp and video.

1. The system map

Read it top to bottom: people reach the app, the app reads and writes the database, and the app calls outside services when it needs money, files or messages.

2. How a stranger becomes a paid learner

This is the lead journey. Each step writes a milestone on the same lead record, so you can always see where a person is.

  1. Attention: they click your linkEach link carries a source tag, for example src=instagram. The click is counted. Nothing posts for you; you share the links yourself.
  2. Capture: they fill a formPattern Check, free resource or masterclass form. Name, email and WhatsApp consent create one lead in the CRM, joined by email so there are no duplicates. You get an alert. Half-filled forms are saved too.
  3. Free masterclass: they reserve a seatThey get the join link by email, then reminders on WhatsApp at 24 hours, 3 hours, 1 hour and 10 minutes. The class opens in a built-in video room. After the class, attendees and no-shows get different follow-ups.
  4. Discovery call: they book a timeYou see a card with their Pattern Check result before you speak. Reminders go the day before and 2 hours before. A no-show gets a reschedule message within 15 minutes.
  5. Programme: they pay and learnRazorpay takes the payment, the learner portal opens, and a welcome message and an invoice go out. Abandoned checkouts get reminders at 1, 24 and 72 hours. On completion the system issues a verifiable certificate.

5. What happens when someone pays

Step 7 verifies the signature on the server, so a buyer cannot fake a payment. A webhook from Razorpay repeats the same check as a safety net. Refunds from the admin or coach screen call Razorpay and mark the sale and the lead as refunded.

6. KYC before withdrawals

  1. Creator submitsName on PAN, PAN number, date of birth (18 or older), address, PAN card and ID proof. The documents are stored privately.
  2. Admin assigns a reviewerReviewers are added by email in the admin panel.
  3. Reviewer checks the documentsOnly they and the admin can open the files.
  4. Reviewer recommendsApprove for final review or request corrections, with remarks. The name and time are recorded.
  5. Admin decidesApprove, reject or ask for resubmission, with a reason the creator can read. Withdrawals stay locked until approval.

7. The daily scheduler

Reminders, follow-ups and the Monday report do not run on their own. A scheduler calls /api/cron/run with the CRON_SECRET. Each call finds every workflow step that is due, checks that the person has not already reached the goal, sends the message on the right channel, and writes the result to the message log with its open and click tracking. Without the scheduler, no timed message is sent.

8. Going live: what still needs setup

On Vercel

NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL, ADMIN_EMAILS, the FIREBASE_*, CLOUDINARY_*, RAZORPAY_*, EmailJS and Twilio variables, and CRON_SECRET. The System health page lists what is missing.

Razorpay

Swap the test keys for live keys and add the webhook address /api/webhooks/razorpay after your business is verified.

WhatsApp

Twilio's sandbox only reaches people who join it. Messaging strangers needs a Meta-approved number and templates.

Not automated

Posting to social media, ads, and Instagram comment-to-DM. The system tracks the clicks and leads they bring.
